<h2>Ever wanted to quickly check if your Wi-fi is down while on your phone? <br> Well now you can!</h2>
<p>Introducing: Is my wifi down? <br>Its very simple to use! Just go to our site https://lukezzi.github.io/ismywifidown/ and you can find out if your wifi is down! <br> If the site loads you get an message saying "Your Wi-fi is not down!" <br> If the page loads then your wifi seems to be down!</p>
